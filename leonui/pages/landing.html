Questions people actually ask

Do my clients need an account?
No. They get a payment link and a PDF. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to remember.
What happens after the 14-day trial?
Nothing automatic. Your account drops to the free Solo plan and keeps working for up to three clients.
Can I change the reminder wording?
Every template is editable, and each escalation step can be turned off on its own. The tone is yours.
Does it work outside the UK?
Yes — 30 currencies and the tax rules for the EU, US, Canada and Australia. Bank transfer and card are supported everywhere we operate.
How do I get my data out?
One click, any time: CSV, or the accountant format of your choice. No export fee and no lock-in.
